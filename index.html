Before you begin

  1. This test has 20 multiple-choice items on the DepEd national assessments: PEPT, NAT, ELLNA, CB-NCAE, PBTS, PISA and SEA-PLM.
  2. You have 25 minutes. The timer starts when you press Start. When time runs out, your answers are submitted automatically.
  3. Choose your level (Elementary or Secondary) and enter the access code given by the facilitator.
  4. Choose one answer per item. You may go back and change answers before you submit.
  5. Items and choices appear in a random order. Do not use notes, slides or other devices.
  6. After you submit, you will see your score and the correct answers with short explanations.
  7. This is a diagnostic. It is not graded and does not affect your standing in the training.
